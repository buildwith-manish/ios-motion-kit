## Notes
- Thresholds: every colour role is held to 4.5:1 (stricter than the 3:1 large-text rule); karaoke dimmed word 3:1; active word 4.5:1. `requiredRatio(sizePx, bold)` is exported for the large-text rule.
- `ensureContrast` is the specified logic plus one fallback: if the chosen end (white/black) cannot reach the target on a mid-tone background, it uses the other end.
- `safeText` picks one direction (white or black) per role so it never oscillates, and leaves unreachable backdrops as FAIL rows.
- Karaoke: dimmed word = primary at `karaokeInactiveOpacity(theme)` (lowest opacity >= 0.4 reaching 3:1 on every surface/backdrop).
- tsc: clean after renaming my `toHex` to `rgbToHex` (name clash with another kit export).

## NOT DONE (needs a decision)
1. **Accent text on the accent orb (midnight, mono; light on canvas).** Text coloured `accent` must beat its own 0.45 orb composite. For a bright accent this is mathematically impossible (white accent: max about 4.3:1; mint #3DF0A8 on its orb about 3.4:1), and lightening the accent brightens the orb, which moves the target. I froze the midnight and mono accents at their original values and stopped after 3 adjustments. Fix options: add a separate `accentText` token, or exclude accent text from the orb backdrop rows.
2. **mono, all light text roles on the accent@.45 orb (#7A7A7C).** That composite has luminance about 0.195, where white tops out below 4.5:1 and only black passes, so no light text colour can pass it together with the dark backdrops. Fix by changing the mono accent (a white orb is the cause) or the 0.45 orb alpha in the backdrop list.
3. **Hierarchy collapse (not a FAIL, but a consequence).** Because every role must reach 4.5:1 on the mint/accent orb composites, midnight primary/secondary/tertiary are now nearly the same near-white, and the semantic colours are pale tints. Same effect in mono (secondary = tertiary = #E4E4E4) and in light (secondary = tertiary, very dark). Darker orb tints or a lower orb alpha in the worst-case list would bring the hierarchy back. Revert is one `git revert` of the theme token change if you prefer the old look.
